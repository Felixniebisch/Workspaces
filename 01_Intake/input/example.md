**Purpose of the presentation:** Inform the leadership team about the Q3 customer survey and propose two actions for Q4.
**Audience:** Leadership team of Fiktive GmbH (five people, technical background, short on time).

## Notes from the survey team

- The survey ran from 1 to 20 September and reached 1,240 customers. 412 answered, a response rate of about 33 %.
- Overall satisfaction rose from 3.8 to 4.1 on a 5-point scale compared with Q2.
- The lowest-rated area is invoicing. 27 % of respondents said invoices were unclear. Source: survey sheet, tab "Invoicing", row 14.
- Customers who used the new support chat rated their issue resolved more often (81 % vs. 64 %). Source: support log export, 3 Sept to 18 Sept.
